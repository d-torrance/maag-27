## About the meeting

The **Meeting on Applied Algebraic Geometry (MAAG)** is a regional gathering
that attracts participants primarily from the Southeastern United States. MAAG
2027 will be held at the [Georgia Tech School of Mathematics](https://math.gatech.edu)
in Atlanta from **Friday, April 16 through Sunday, April 18, 2027**.

The meeting brings together researchers who use algebraic geometry as a tool —
in optimization, statistics, numerical computation, combinatorics,
cryptography, biology, and beyond — along with graduate students and postdocs
entering the field. Talks are aimed at a broad audience.

## What to expect

- **Invited talks** by researchers across applied algebraic geometry.
  See [Talks]({{ '/talks/' | relative_url }}).
- **A [Macaulay2 mini-workshop]({{ '/workshop/' | relative_url }})** with
  hands-on sessions, opening the meeting on Friday afternoon.
- **A [poster session]({{ '/posters/' | relative_url }})** on Saturday
  afternoon, preceded by a poster blitz. Graduate students and postdocs are
  especially encouraged to present.

## Registration and travel support

Registration is free, but we ask everyone to register so we can plan meals and
space. **[NSF]({{ '/funding/' | relative_url }}) travel support is available**,
with priority given to graduate students, postdocs, and early-career
researchers without other funding.
