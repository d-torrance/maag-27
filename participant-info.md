## Venue

All sessions are held in the **Skiles Building**, home of the Georgia Tech
School of Mathematics:

> Skiles Classroom Building
> 686 Cherry Street NW
> Atlanta, GA 30332

## Getting to Atlanta

**By air.** Hartsfield-Jackson Atlanta International Airport (**ATL**) is the
nearest airport and is served by essentially every domestic carrier.

**From the airport by transit.** ATL has a
[MARTA](https://www.itsmarta.com) rail station inside the domestic terminal —
you do not need a shuttle. Board any **northbound** train and ride to **North
Avenue Station** (about 20 minutes). Exit the station to the south, walk west on
North Avenue across the interstate, turn right onto Cherry Street, pass through
the vehicle barrier, and Skiles is the second building on the left.

Midtown Station is an alternative; from there Georgia Tech's free
[Stinger shuttles](https://www.pts.gatech.edu/shuttles/stinger/) connect to
campus. Routes and hours vary by day and by semester — check the Stinger page
closer to the meeting, and note that weekend service differs from weekdays.

**By car.** Georgia Tech is just west of the I-75/I-85 connector in Midtown
Atlanta, immediately north of downtown.

## Lodging

**Lodging details will be posted here.** Georgia Tech works with several hotels
in Midtown Atlanta for visitors, and we have not yet finalized which one MAAG
2027 will use. Once a hotel and room block are confirmed, we will post the
booking link, the conference rate, and the cutoff date on this page and email
registered participants.

If you plan to book independently in the meantime, look for hotels in
**Midtown** or **Technology Square**, both within walking distance or a short
transit ride of the Skiles Building.

## Meals

Coffee and pastries are provided each morning, and there is a reception during
Saturday's poster session. Lunch on Saturday is on your own — there are many
options in Technology Square and along West Peachtree Street, a short walk from
Skiles. We will circulate suggestions closer to the meeting.

Please note any dietary restrictions on the registration form.

## Accessibility

The Skiles Building is wheelchair accessible. If you have accessibility
requirements — mobility, dietary, childcare, or anything else that would help
you participate fully — please tell us on the registration form or email
[{{ site.event.contact_email }}](mailto:{{ site.event.contact_email }}). We will
do our best to accommodate requests made in advance.
