We describe a witness-set construction adapted to sparse polynomial systems,
where the ambient space is a toric compactification determined by the Newton
polytopes rather than $$\mathbb{P}^n$$. An implementation is available in the
Macaulay2 package `NumericalAlgebraicGeometry`.
