We study toric degenerations of $$\mathrm{Gr}(2,n)$$ arising from
maximal-dimensional cones of the tropical Grassmannian, and describe an
algorithm implemented in *Macaulay2* for computing the associated
Newton--Okounkov bodies. As an application we recover the string polytopes of
$$\mathrm{SL}_n$$ and give a new proof that they are lattice polytopes.

Display equations work too:

$$
\dim \mathrm{Gr}(2,n) = 2(n-2).
$$
